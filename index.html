How the booking week works
  • Attendance booking. Employees pick a building and day from real-time availability — no double-booking.
  • Cancel booking. One-tap cancellation frees the slot instantly for someone else — capacity stays accurate.
  • Book a week ahead. Reservations are made for the following week, never the current one — the window closes Thursday 5 PM and reopens Friday 2 PM.
  • Weekly report available. A report of the current week's reservations is available to the business.
  • Grade 13 and above are excluded from the reservation system.
